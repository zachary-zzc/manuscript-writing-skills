# Checks for specialised scientific claims

Use only the matching examples when an audit or requested peer review depends on a specialised measurement, validation regime or operating condition. These are prompts to inspect evidence, not universal reporting requirements or instructions to commission new experiments. Start from the actual claim and retained sources; classify a missing explanation separately from a demonstrated invalid analysis. Keep existing reviewer independence and findings records.

- **Chemical or materials mechanisms:** distinguish evidence for identity/composition, the measured property and the proposed mechanism. Check whether the characterisation or discriminating control supports the asserted link; an associated readout alone may support a narrower interpretation. Compare performance under the actual normalisation and operating conditions, not a headline value detached from its basis.
- **Engineered systems and devices:** separate calibration, optimisation and design selection from independent validation. Trace deployment, autonomy, durability or scale claims to the demonstrated operating range. A simulation or selected demonstration does not alone establish performance across unseen physical conditions.
- **Environmental and spatial products:** identify the measured or retrieved variable before assessing the inferred quantity. For example, river stage and discharge require a justified relationship; total water storage is not interchangeable with groundwater storage. Inspect the conversion, spatial/temporal support and uncertainty when such an inference is central.
- **Spatial or temporal prediction:** inspect how nearby sites, repeated periods and shared sensors enter development and validation sets. A nominal split can retain dependence or leakage; assess independence against the claimed generalisation, not the split label. Check whether product/version changes, aggregation or temporal sampling could explain an apparent trend.
- **Ecological or management implications:** distinguish a proxy from the ecosystem property claimed, and check whether observations represent the sites, seasons or taxa to which the conclusion extends. Keep stock and flux quantities, units and time bases distinct. A local association does not alone establish an intervention's effect at a policy scale.

Use these examples to locate the relevant evidence, assess credible alternatives and calibrate the claim. Do not infer a flaw solely because a particular method or extra control is absent. Explain any concern through the affected conclusion and a concrete repair, which may be clearer reporting or narrower wording; additional analysis remains separately authorized.
