# Manuscript Polishing

Read the [shared manuscript contract](../manuscript-workflow/references/contract.md) directly. Reuse the current mode, accepted storyline, authoritative wording and protected content; a local edit does not restart manuscript discovery. If the request names a manuscript section, use that section's focused skill and its applicable quality gates instead of broadening this task.

## Hard rules for manuscript narrative

Apply the **Editorial Guideline (mandatory default)** in [release narrative and defensive-writing rules](../manuscript-workflow/references/release-narrative-rules.md) before drafting, restructuring, polishing, auditing, or reviewing. They are hard defaults, with special priority for manuscript writing, storyline, idea framing, and innovation claims. When the user supplies manuscript text, perform the required point-by-point defensive-writing audit and give a concrete rewrite for every instance. Preserve material limitations, negative findings, uncertainty, ethics, safety, and reproducibility boundaries as precise evidence or scope statements.

## Journal-specific polishing

For Nature or Nature Portfolio targets, keep `manuscript-polishing` as the canonical polishing owner. Load the relevant Nature guidance from [`nature-shared/core`](../nature-shared/core/) only when the target section or journal requirement needs it, and verify current official journal instructions for exact limits or submission rules. Do not start a second journal-specific polishing workflow. Preserve the general contract, accepted contribution, evidence boundaries and author decisions when applying journal-specific style.

Read the [shared writing style](../manuscript-workflow/references/writing-style.md) and [paragraph review](../manuscript-workflow/references/paragraph-review.md). Inspect the passage, neighbouring transitions and necessary sources. Preserve facts, numbers, scientific distinctions, citation support, formal notation and claim strength through polishing or translation. Do not invent a missing premise; narrow unsupported wording or identify the needed fact.

Repair logic and scope before surface style. Keep precise technical terms consistent, make referents clear, and remove unnecessary wording without deleting evidence, necessary qualifications or explanatory connections. Review every sentence and adjacent pair within scope and repeat those checks on the actual replacement, including its boundaries with neighbouring prose.

Deliver the requested revised prose in the agreed language and format. Apply source edits only within existing authorization, preserve unaffected text, and inspect affected compiled/rendered output when saved sources change and tools are available. Report a consequential change in meaning or unresolved author fact; ordinary wording fixes do not create new approval stops. Use the contract's mode-specific review and feedback-learning rules.
