### Supplementary Information and references

Organise SI around main claims and their supporting evidence. Inventory relevant notes, figures, panels, tables, protocols, prompts, rubrics, administered questionnaires and source data. Preserve instruments faithfully. Explain central scientific meaning in main text and provide supporting depth here. Remove engineering diaries, needless paths, duplicate Discussions, orphan panels and unnecessary duplicates of main figures. Reconcile callouts, appearance order, printed numbering and assets.

Apply the accepted Results allocation rather than treating every robustness, subgroup or negative result as supplementary by default. Keep evidence that changes the central interpretation visible in the main text, with supporting detail here when appropriate. Give retained supporting analyses stable main-text pointers and preserve their conditions and inferential definitions. For source-data or availability work, use the shared [data availability checks](../../manuscript-audit/references/data-availability.md); ordinary SI prose does not require a new repository audit.

[Apply the shared reference rules](../../manuscript-workflow/references/writing-style.md#references) to citations and bibliography entries.

**Gate:** evidence is complete across the package, citations support their claims and all references resolve. Unknown author facts remain explicit; recoverable scientific placeholders are resolved from evidence.
