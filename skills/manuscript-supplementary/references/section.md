### Supplementary Information and references

Organise SI around main claims and their supporting evidence. In full-manuscript production this is the independent H9S deliverable, not an optional appendix to Methods. Inventory relevant notes, figures, panels, tables, protocols, prompts, rubrics, administered questionnaires, source data, negative findings and robustness analyses. Preserve instruments faithfully. Explain central scientific meaning in main text and provide supporting depth here. Remove engineering diaries, needless paths, duplicate Discussions, orphan panels and unnecessary duplicates of main figures. Reconcile callouts, appearance order, printed numbering and assets.

Apply the accepted Results allocation rather than treating every robustness, subgroup or negative result as supplementary by default. Keep evidence that changes the central interpretation visible in the main text, with supporting detail here when appropriate. Give every retained supporting analysis, figure and table a stable SI identifier and a main-text callout. If an item has no callout, record an evidence-based N/A reason and remove it from the active SI. Preserve conditions and inferential definitions. For source-data or availability work, use the shared [data availability checks](../../manuscript-audit/references/data-availability.md); ordinary SI prose does not require a new repository audit.

[Apply the shared reference rules](../../manuscript-workflow/references/writing-style.md#references) to citations and bibliography entries.

**Gate:** the SI inventory, outline, source files, compiled SI output, page inspection, supplementary figure/table audit, citations, cross-references, callouts, numbering and assets are complete and consistent with the main text. Evidence is complete across the package and all references resolve. Unknown author facts remain explicit; recoverable scientific placeholders are resolved from evidence.
