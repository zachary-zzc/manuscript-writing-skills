# Scientific storyline and Results architecture

Apply the [shared manuscript contract](../../manuscript-workflow/references/contract.md). This reference owns the framework record and H1–H4; section production and whole-manuscript sequencing are handled by their focused owners.

## Maintain one storyline sheet

Keep one current sheet alongside a sustained manuscript project, rather than a new report for every routine pass. For a brief chat-only edit, the conversation can hold the relevant record; no separate file or scaffold is required. Record the applicable details:

1. Scientific question and one accurate main message.
2. Principal discovery versus method/resource contribution, their support, novelty and intended emphasis. A methods paper need not add a separate application-domain discovery; do not force coequal findings into a false single-result claim.
3. Motivating drawbacks: precise scope, primary references and scientific consequence.
4. Ordered findings, evidence dependencies and transitions.
5. Main displays, panel purposes and supporting SI.
6. Integrated scientific meaning, material boundaries, terminology decisions, accepted decisions and open questions.

Use the argument spine **scientific need → real unresolved question → central advance/discovery → supporting findings → scientific meaning**. This is an argument structure, not a fixed paragraph sequence. Order evidence by scientific dependency and importance, not component inventories, work chronology or plot-generation order.

## Framework record and term-table gate

For a new or full manuscript, H1–H4 produce one framework record before figure production or Results prose. Keep the record in the existing storyline/decision record rather than creating a parallel tracker. It must contain:

- the scientific question, one accurate main message, contribution type and evidence boundary
- the supported motivating drawbacks and claim–source links
- the ordered Results architecture, permitted inferences, transitions and main/SI allocation
- the claim–evidence–display map and unresolved dependencies
- one canonical terminology table using the fields in [paragraph-review.md](../../manuscript-workflow/references/paragraph-review.md#1-terminology-table)
- decision status, artifact version, owner and the next ready unit

The term table is a working artifact, not a list of style preferences. H1–H4 establish canonical terms, referents, analytical units, definitions, scope, variants and distinctions. H8–H11 update its locations when new prose, displays or SI introduce a term. H12 freezes the table against the compiled main text, captions, tables, artwork and SI. Do not begin H5–H7 production with an incomplete framework record or an unreviewed term-table entry that affects interpretation.

## Discovery and argument checkpoints

Read actual results and sources before recommendations. Prepare concrete evidence-backed artifacts before requesting a decision; do not ask the author to invent the story from an empty template.

| Checkpoint | Artifact and decision |
|---|---|
| **H1 Discovery + contribution** | Recommend the headline finding, decisive evidence, meaning and novelty status. Distinguish findings from method/resource contributions. Show alternatives only where supported. Confirm what the paper establishes and what leads. |
| **H2 Motivating drawbacks** | Map each problem to primary evidence, scope, consequence, proposed advance and the investigation or argument addressing it. Separate unresolved facts from established limitations. Confirm real, relevant and fairly stated problems. |
| **H3 Whole-paper storyline** | Present the complete framework record, including the storyline sheet, term table, logical steps, evidence dependencies, transitions, integrated conclusion, accepted decisions and unresolved facts. Complete the framework before detailed expansion. |
| **H4 Results architecture** | Present ordered conclusive headings. For each: question, finding, story role, evidence/comparison, permitted inference, main display, SI, term-table dependencies and transition. Freeze the framework and term table before H5–H7 production. |

H2 concerns prior approaches and unresolved scientific needs; track this study's limitations separately in interpretation and Discussion. Manufacture neither to fill a template. Prepare the plan from existing retained evidence; planning does not authorize new experiments or historical raw-data changes.

## Review under the selected mode

Easy performs applicable H1–H4 internally and records autonomous decisions as agent-selected, never author-confirmed merely because no question was asked.

| Checkpoint | Normal review | Expert review |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Principal finding, contribution, claim scope. | Candidate findings, evidence, novelty and interpretation. |
| H2 | Supported drawback map, optionally with H3. | Each drawback, citation, consequence and experimental connection. |
| H3 | Complete framework record, storyline, term table and main message. | Each logical step, transition, term-table distinction and meaningful framing alternative. |
| H4 | Results order, headings, focus, decisive evidence and main/SI allocation. | Each subsection's question, conclusion, design, comparison, term-table dependency and role. |

Normal may combine packets while preserving each discovery, drawback, storyline and Results-architecture decision. Present the recommendation, actual artifact, evidence, precise consequential choice and next authorized work. Use the [terminology and continuity checks](../../manuscript-workflow/references/paragraph-review.md) when framing the story; include consequential decisions in these checkpoints rather than adding separate approval stops for routine repairs.

## Existing argument and handoff

For a changed headline finding or whole-paper restructuring, revisit affected H1–H4 decisions. For Results reorganisation with an accepted discovery, use H3/H4 and verify evidence without redundant reapproval. A local correction retains the accepted story and checks affected dependencies. A full/substantive whole-manuscript revision first completes the [whole-manuscript audit](../../manuscript-audit/references/audit.md#whole-manuscript-structure-and-paragraph-audit).

Test each planned Results subsection: does it advance, support or materially bound the central claim, have concrete evidence, and occupy the right place in the sequence? Move peripheral material to SI or integrate it where appropriate. Preserve important null results and qualifications; storyline emphasis must not suppress evidence. Do not force one drawback, module and subsection into a one-to-one mapping. Record main/SI allocation, story role, permitted inference and transition for each finding; do not force coequal findings into a false single-result claim.

For a substantive Results plan or reorganisation, make each subsection's contribution testable: what becomes supportable here, and what inference, indispensable support or material boundary would disappear if this subsection were removed? Compare adjacent answers. Reusing a baseline is legitimate; presenting the same comparison and conclusion as another discovery is not. Combine overlapping demonstrations or identify the distinct question, competing explanation, scope or boundary resolved by the later evidence. Record that distinction in the existing H4 entry. Necessary support need not establish a separate discovery, and the argument need not follow a fixed progression towards mechanism or causality. Use only retained evidence; a missing discriminating test is a gap, not permission to run it.

The accepted plan is a baseline for section drafting, not automatic acceptance of future prose. Hand Results production to [manuscript-results](../../manuscript-results/SKILL.md) and visual planning to [manuscript-figures](../../manuscript-figures/SKILL.md). Preserve accepted decisions, author-requested writing order and unresolved facts. Reopen a finding, drawback, conclusion, order or comparison only when new evidence or dependent changes materially affect it, under the selected mode.
