# Manuscript Storyline

Read the [shared manuscript contract](../manuscript-workflow/references/contract.md) directly and reuse the continuing task's mode, sources, accepted decisions and scope. Do not invoke the coordinator or restart mode selection when these are established.

## Hard rules for manuscript narrative

Apply the **Editorial Guideline (mandatory default)** in [release narrative and defensive-writing rules](../manuscript-workflow/references/release-narrative-rules.md) before drafting, restructuring, polishing, auditing, or reviewing. They are hard defaults, with special priority for manuscript writing, storyline, idea framing, and innovation claims. When the user supplies manuscript text, perform the required point-by-point defensive-writing audit and give a concrete rewrite for every instance. Preserve material limitations, negative findings, uncertainty, ethics, safety, and reproducibility boundaries as precise evidence or scope statements.

For Nature or Nature Portfolio targets, follow the [canonical Nature integration map](../manuscript-workflow/references/nature-integration.md) and load only the relevant `nature-shared` reference; this manuscript skill remains the owner.

Read [planning.md](references/planning.md) for H1–H4: discovery/contribution, supported motivating drawbacks, the whole-paper argument and ordered Results architecture. Inspect the actual evidence before framing the contribution. Preserve important null findings and distinguish methodological contributions from application-domain discoveries; never invent an experiment or discovery to complete the plan.

Maintain one current storyline/decision record, using the conversation for a brief task. Prepare concrete evidence-backed review artifacts, then apply the contract's mode to the planning checkpoints. In Easy record supported choices as agent-selected. In Normal/Expert preserve each consequential decision even when review packets are combined.

For a full or substantive whole-manuscript revision, complete the [structure and paragraph audit](../manuscript-audit/references/audit.md#whole-manuscript-structure-and-paragraph-audit) before rewriting. A bounded framing correction preserves accepted decisions and checks only affected dependencies. Deliver the question, contribution, drawback/evidence map, ordered findings, permitted inferences, main/SI map and unresolved author facts at the requested scale. Hand accepted section plans to the relevant section skill without repeating H1–H4 unless their meaning changes.
