# Whole-manuscript coordination

For current-state assessment, resuming work or selecting the next action, use [workflow-state.md](workflow-state.md). For new or full-manuscript work, H0–H12 define mandatory dependency gates. Preparation may run in parallel where prerequisites allow, but no applicable gate may be silently skipped.

Read this guide for a complete manuscript or revision spanning sections. For a bounded section task, use its focused skill directly. The coordinator selects the next owner; the shared contract controls review and evidence boundaries.

## Confirm the mode once

The [shared contract](contract.md#confirm-the-mode-once) owns mode selection and persistence. Read it directly once per continuing task; skill maintenance needs no manuscript mode.

Use [manuscript-storyline](../../manuscript-storyline/SKILL.md) and its [planning guide](../../manuscript-storyline/references/planning.md#maintain-one-storyline-sheet). Keep one current record and preserve accepted decisions across section handoffs.

## New manuscript: H0–H12

Use manuscript-storyline for H1–H4, manuscript-figures for H5–H6, manuscript-results for H7, manuscript-introduction for H8, manuscript-methods for H9M, manuscript-supplementary for H9S, manuscript-discussion for H10, manuscript-abstract for H11, and the coordinator plus manuscript-audit for H12. Resolve these through the [owner table](../SKILL.md#select-the-owner).

Read actual results and sources before recommendations. Prepare concrete evidence-backed artifacts before requesting a decision; do not ask the author to invent the story from an empty template.

| Checkpoint | Artifact and decision |
|---|---|
| **H0 Intake and dependency gate** | Lock article type, target/format/template, authoritative evidence inventory, interaction mode, framework-record scaffold, term-table scaffold, claim–source ledger scaffold, visual/SI allocation scaffold and output/package plan. Mark each item PASS, BLOCKED or N/A with a reason. |
| **H1 Discovery + contribution** | Complete the framework record's question, main message, principal discovery or method/resource contribution, evidence boundary, novelty status and intended emphasis. Distinguish findings from method/resource contributions. Show alternatives only where supported. |
| **H2 Motivating drawbacks** | Map each problem to primary evidence, scope, consequence, proposed advance and the investigation or argument addressing it. Separate unresolved facts from established limitations. Confirm real, relevant and fairly stated problems. |
| **H3 Whole-paper storyline** | Present the complete framework record, including the storyline sheet, term table, logical steps, evidence dependencies, transitions, integrated conclusion, accepted decisions and unresolved facts. Complete the framework before detailed expansion. |
| **H4 Results architecture** | Present ordered conclusive headings. For each: question, finding, story role, evidence/comparison, permitted inference, main display, SI, term-table dependencies and transition. Freeze the framework and term table before H5–H7 production. |
| **H5 Visual architecture** | Storyboard main/SI figures and tables in order. Specify each claim, panel questions, real data sources, comparison conditions, encoding, takeaway and canonical terminology. Use the shared term table for panel labels, captions, table headers and callouts. Identify author artwork and authentic scored outputs. Confirm the evidence needed to tell the story. |
| **H6 Real visual prototypes** | Produce representative panels and draft legends using canonical terms from the shared table; offer alternatives only for consequential uncertainty. Check fidelity/readability before review. Confirm communication and visual language, then extend the accepted approach appropriately. |
| **H7 Results subsection, repeated** | Present a complete subsection, any supporting display/legend, necessary linked Methods/SI changes and material unresolved facts. Show its story role and paragraph logic. Confirm conclusion, evidence sufficiency, explanation, significance and visual alignment where displays are present. |
| **H8 Introduction** | Present its paragraph skeleton, then complete prose in context. Verify drawbacks, supporting examples, transitions and closing study overview. Confirm that the argument prepares readers for the actual evidence. |
| **H9M Methods** | Present topical structure and real procedures, data, historical configurations, metric/statistical definitions, necessary equations and the main/SI division. Resolve author facts and scientific completeness without engineering-documentation drift. |
| **H9S Supplementary Information** | Present the SI inventory and outline, supporting analyses, protocols, source data, supplementary figures/tables, negative or robustness results, captions, references, assets and main-text pointers. Use the shared term table for SI headings, labels and captions. Compile and inspect the SI as an independent deliverable. Every retained item has a main-text callout or an evidence-based N/A reason. |
| **H10 Discussion** | Present a function-based interpretation outline and full prose aligned with Introduction and Results: central advance, synthesis and prior work, claim-specific limitations and a closing scientific consequence, allocated to the actual argument. Confirm meaning and material boundaries. |
| **H11 Abstract + title** | Present complete candidates expressing the accepted problem, contribution, principal evidence and significance. Check headline claims against the body. Confirm representation and emphasis. |
| **H12 Integrated package** | Assemble/audit main text, SI, displays, legends, references and supporting material. Correct authorized routine issues; compile and inspect the output. Present scientific decisions and separate readiness states; confirm the intended final version where the mode requires. |

### Required production sequence

1. Complete H0 and record all mandatory dependencies, owners and blocked inputs. Resolve each dependency at its own gate rather than treating H0 as a substitute for H1–H12.
2. Complete H1–H4 from actual evidence, including the framework record, terminology table, claim–evidence map, permitted inferences and ordered Results architecture. Do not start figure production or Results prose before this record is complete for the selected mode.
3. Complete H5 by mapping every core claim to a panel, retained data/artwork, encoding, caption and main/SI destination.
4. Complete H6 with representative real-data prototypes. Iterate geometry, typography, encodings, captions and source fidelity before extending the design.
5. Complete H7 by writing Results around the accepted figures, then revise Results and figures together until claim–evidence–display alignment passes.
6. Complete H8 Introduction from the stabilized problem, literature ledger, contribution and Results.
7. Complete H9M Methods from actual procedures, records and the stabilized Results.
8. Complete H9S Supplementary Information as an independent evidence package. Apply the same figure gates to supplementary figures and reconcile every main-text pointer, number, citation and asset.
9. Complete H10 Discussion from the accepted findings, figure interpretation, Methods and SI-supported limitations.
10. Complete H11 Abstract and title only after the body, figures and SI stabilize.
11. Complete H12 with the full main/SI package and multi-pass audit.

Methods records may be inventoried earlier, but final Methods prose follows the stabilized Results. SI preparation may begin from the H0 inventory, but H9S must be completed and independently verified after the Results and figure set stabilize. An explicitly requested provisional section must be labelled provisional and cannot satisfy its final gate. H2 concerns prior approaches and unresolved scientific needs; track this study's limitations separately in interpretation and H10. Manufacture neither evidence nor display to fill a template.

## Existing manuscript routing

Identify the authoritative source, accepted decisions, requested scope and protected content. For full or substantive manuscript revision, complete the [whole-manuscript structure and paragraph audit](../../manuscript-audit/references/audit.md#whole-manuscript-structure-and-paragraph-audit) before rewriting. Reconstruct the present argument first. For major restructuring compare current/proposed discovery, drawbacks, argument, Results structure, displays and dependent sections. Explicitly bounded local wording/layout edits retain targeted verification; follow the applicable route below after the audit when a full revision is in scope.

- Changed headline finding or whole-paper restructuring: H1–H4, then visual/subsection work.
- Results reorganisation with accepted discovery: H3/H4; verify support without demanding redundant reapproval.
- One substantive subsection: H7; revisit earlier decisions only if materially changed.
- Figure-only revision: H5/H6, full relevant visual checks and caption/text synchronization.
- Introduction drawback revision: H2/H8; inspect effects on Results claims.
- Local wording/layout: preserve the accepted story and use targeted verification within authorization.
- Audit-only: findings and concrete revision proposals; no automatic manuscript edits, raw-record changes or new experiments.
- Post-review: use [submission-and-revision.md](submission-and-revision.md); preserve original reports and map comments to evidence, action, completion status, location and response. Apply relevant checkpoints to substantive changes; reviewer requests alone do not authorize experiments.

## Decisions, audit and completion

Present each required checkpoint with a recommendation, artifact, evidence, precise decision and next authorized work. Accept/revise/defer applies to the identified scope. Decisions persist across sessions. Deferral holds only dependent work; silence is not approval. In Normal/Expert, reopen a material finding, drawback, Results conclusion, order, comparison or figure interpretation only when new evidence/dependent changes warrant it; show affected story elements together. Easy resolves supported choices and reports unresolved dependencies without asking.

Run the multi-pass audit during production and at H12. Verify one coherent argument across title, abstract, Introduction, Results, displays, Discussion and SI; Methods must describe actual procedures and SI must supply meaningful, traceable support. Numerical errors, false labels, unsupported mechanisms, invented data, orphan SI items and defensive-language failures are not optional presentation choices.

Report scientific support, terminology and defensive-language review, numerical reproduction, visual fidelity/readability, caption alignment, export/package verification, compilation and applicable venue compliance separately. Normal/Expert present the actual inspected package for final review; Easy performs the same checks internally and reports unresolved gates. Apply the feedback-learning contract in [learning.md](learning.md).
