# Shared manuscript contract

Read this contract directly from the selected manuscript skill, once per continuing task. Switching sections or helpers does not restart intake. Explicit author instructions govern scope, format and review waivers. Skill maintenance and workflow discussion do not require a manuscript mode.

For sustained work coordinated by manuscript-workflow, return the current unit, artifact/version, decision and source-application status, completed checks, unresolved dependencies and next action to the existing project record. The [workflow manager](workflow-state.md) chooses the next owner when needed. A straightforward bounded section task need not load the management guide or create a tracker.

## Scope and evidence

Keep the scientific question, accepted storyline, actual evidence and author intent aligned across disciplines and languages. Conditional rules about quantitative outcomes, prediction, biology, agents or people apply only when those features exist in the study. Do not add experiments, models, datasets, participants, scores or instruments to satisfy an example. A methodological contribution need not include an application-domain discovery.

Use authoritative sources and preserve protected prose, artwork, raw records and evaluated outputs. Prior assistant drafts and author hypotheses are not evidence. Manuscript work does not itself authorize new experiments, historical raw-data changes or external submission. Keep one current record of scope, sources, target, language, mode, scientific message, evidence boundaries, terminology, accepted decisions and unresolved facts. Reuse an existing project record; conversation context suffices for a brief edit.

For bounded section or paragraph work, preserve the accepted argument and unaffected material. Full or substantive whole-manuscript revision requires the [manuscript audit](../../manuscript-audit/SKILL.md) before rewriting. Do not turn a bounded edit into that full lifecycle. Audit-only requests remain audits.

## Confirm the mode once

Before substantive work on a new manuscript or a new revision task, obtain an interaction-mode choice. If none exists, explain Easy, Normal and Expert concisely and wait before drafting, revising or producing figures. Normal may be recommended; never silently select it. An explicit selection already confirms the mode: acknowledge it without asking again. Reuse the confirmed mode throughout the continuing task, including audits and context changes. Record scope, mode, decisions and pending items in the existing working record. A new task requires a choice unless the author established a standing preference.

- **Easy:** complete the authorized workflow autonomously, including drafting, figures, audits, corrections and delivery. Ask no ordinary manuscript-confirmation, final-acceptance or proactive skill-upgrade questions.
- **Normal:** confirm essential scientific and presentation decisions. Review substantive Results subsections as complete units; group related decisions into concrete packets. Handle ordinary prose, layout and consistency fixes autonomously.
- **Expert:** use every applicable checkpoint at the necessary section, paragraph, figure/panel and legend granularity. Proceed paragraph by paragraph and section by section unless the author explicitly agrees to a batch.

These are writing-review modes. Expert mode does not imply that the research includes expert participants, human assessment or an intervention condition.

Quality is identical in all modes. Easy performs the full scientific and visual checks internally. Recover missing facts from evidence; otherwise narrow/omit unsupported claims and record unresolved author facts without invention. Deliver completed useful work with an unresolved-items statement; do not call an incomplete scientific package ready.

An explicit switch confirms the new mode. A later instruction such as “show this paragraph before applying it” establishes a local review point without changing the whole mode. Entering Easy removes ordinary workflow review stops for remaining authorized work. Suspend pending learning proposals without saving or blocking manuscript work. Easy does not waive a separate action-specific restriction, such as an instruction to preserve the original source or obtain approval before external submission. Leave that restricted action pending and complete independent authorized work; do not invent such restrictions from the ordinary H1–H12 checkpoints. “Continue” in Expert advances to the next review artifact, not past all remaining reviews. A scoped batch/implementation waiver governs that scope. External submission/publication retains its own authorization.

## Review at the applicable checkpoint

Easy performs all applicable H1–H12 internally. Record autonomous decisions as **agent-selected**, never **author-confirmed** merely because no question was asked.

| Checkpoint | Normal review | Expert review |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Principal finding, contribution, claim scope. | Candidate findings, evidence, novelty and interpretation. |
| H2 | Supported drawback map, optionally with H3. | Each drawback, citation, consequence and experimental connection. |
| H3 | Complete skeleton and message. | Each logical step, transition and meaningful framing alternative. |
| H4 | Order, headings, focus and decisive evidence. | Each subsection's question, conclusion, design, comparison and role. |
| H5 | Main/SI map and principal visual messages. | Every display, panel sequence, allocation, source and comparison. |
| H6 | Representative prototype for a new/materially changed visual approach. | Necessary figures/panels, encodings, layout, interpretation and legends. |
| H7 | Each substantive subsection with displays as a unit; explicit batches allowed. | Plan, individual paragraphs/agreed small batches, panels/legends, completed section. |
| H8 | Outline and complete Introduction. | Outline and paragraph purposes, then the complete prose preview followed by paragraph-by-paragraph confirmation. |
| H9 | Consequential protocol/factual ambiguities and material scientific choices. | Structure, procedures, definitions, equations, statistics and SI section by section; paragraph detail where needed. |
| H10 | Consequential interpretation changes; otherwise with H12. | Argument, paragraphs, prior work, limitations and closing synthesis. |
| H11 | Complete abstract and title choice. | Headline selection, evidence balance, individual sentences and alternatives. |
| H12 | Integrated draft and material remaining decisions. | Whole argument, cross-section consistency, displays, SI and open issues. |

Normal may combine packets while preserving discovery, drawbacks, storyline, Results and visualization decisions. Apply the [terminology and logical-continuity audit](paragraph-review.md#terminology-and-logical-continuity) at the relevant scope before presenting prose in H7–H11 and the integrated H12 packet. Include terminology decisions and consequential paragraph/sentence logic findings in those existing checkpoints; do not add separate approval stops for routine repairs. Expert reviews scientific meaning and communication, not every typo as a new decision.

## Whole-section preview and paragraph review

For substantive section drafting or revision in Expert, prepare the complete requested section and apply the [terminology and logical-continuity audit](paragraph-review.md#terminology-and-logical-continuity) to every sentence, adjacent sentence pair and paragraph boundary before presenting it. Audit the revised wording itself after repairs; an audit of the earlier version does not validate the replacement. Keep preparation comprehensive even when approval proceeds in small units.

Present the full section as readable plain prose for review, without LaTeX commands or a code block, unless the author requests another format. Then seek confirmation of one paragraph at a time, starting with the first unapproved paragraph. Showing the full draft does not approve its later paragraphs or terminology. Preserve accepted decisions, revise the paragraph under review, and recheck its boundaries with neighbouring paragraphs before advancing. Review acceptance and source-file application follow the author's existing authorization.

A request for a bounded local edit, a different review order, source format or an agreed batch overrides this default within that scope. Normal and Easy retain their mode-specific review granularity; do not add paragraph approval stops to Easy.

## Decisions and feedback

Prepare a concrete artifact, recommendation, evidence, consequential choice and next authorized work before each required review. Accept, revise or defer applies to the identified scope. Decisions persist across sessions. Deferral holds only dependent work; silence is not approval. In Normal/Expert, reopen a material finding, drawback, Results conclusion, order, comparison or figure interpretation only when new evidence or dependent changes warrant it. Easy resolves supported choices and reports unresolved dependencies without ordinary approval questions.

For Normal/Expert, read [learning.md](learning.md) when beginning substantive work and assess every substantive human comment for a reusable preference. Apply current-task feedback within authorization; persistent changes are separate. Present an inferred rule's exact text, trigger/exceptions, destination and effect, then ask approve / revise / keep task-specific before saving. A direct request to update a particular skill rule already authorizes that update. Do not generalize project facts, one-off conditions or temporary budgets into universal rules. Easy has no proactive learning prompts or unapproved persistent updates; suspend unanswered proposals when switching to Easy.

Other specialist skills may assist when available, but must not restart intake, add unrequested reviewers or override this contract. Loading a section skill does not require also loading the coordinator's full lifecycle.

## Verify the requested deliverable

Reconcile changed claims, source values, statistical units, comparison conditions and affected text, displays, legends, Methods and SI. For generated displays, separately check scientific/data fidelity, final-size readability, legend/narrative interpretation and export/provenance properties. Preserve original questionnaires, independent ratings and scored images where present.

Compile/render and inspect affected saved manuscript output when tools are available. Text-only consultation does not require a manuscript build. Report scientific support, numerical reproduction, visual checks, compilation, inspected layout, package completeness and verified venue compliance separately; none proves all the others. Never invent a successful check. Easy delivers without final acceptance prompts; Normal/Expert use the applicable final review.
