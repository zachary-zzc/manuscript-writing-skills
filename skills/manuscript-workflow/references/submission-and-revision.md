# Submission materials and reviewer correspondence

Load only for requested submission materials, reviewer correspondence or package checks. The workflow manager coordinates; section owners change the manuscript and manuscript-audit verifies disputed evidence and final consistency. Reuse the confirmed mode and existing work record. This reference adds no approval checkpoints and does not authorize submission, contacting editors/reviewers or new experiments.

## Initial submission

Check the actual journal, article type and submission stage. Classify requested package items as required, optional, not accepted or not applicable from current official instructions. Reuse supplied author metadata, declarations, permissions and manuscript facts; mark missing information precisely. Do not manufacture author approval, contribution roles, conflicts, grant/ethics identifiers, reviewer relationships or repository access.

An initial cover letter identifies the manuscript and article type, explains the supported advance and decisive evidence, and relates it to the journal's readership. Avoid replaying the abstract or inventing priority and significance. Prepare a title page, highlights, reporting checklist or declarations only when requested or required for the authorized package; there is no universal letter length or highlight count. Select reporting guidance from the actual study design and verify its applicability rather than attaching every familiar checklist.

Reconcile names/order, affiliations, title, counts, declarations and data/code statements across files. Where anonymous review applies, inspect the relevant files for identifying content. Keep initial-submission requirements distinct from revision and accepted-manuscript requirements. If reviewer suggestions are requested, verify expertise and public contact details and distinguish known relationships from conflict information still needed from the authors. Missing administrative facts hold the dependent item, not independent drafting.

## Responses and actual changes

Preserve the decision letter and original comments. Record editor instructions and reviewer boundaries without inferring a major/minor decision from tone or workload. An unknown decision may limit package strategy; it does not block a bounded reply edit. The decision label does not downgrade a substantive validity concern.

Use stable comment IDs in the existing record: concern, supporting evidence, proposed action, owner, progress, manuscript location and response. Split compound comments when they need distinct answers while retaining their source identity. Separate planned work, author-reported completion, artifact-verified completion and justified non-action. A response must not say an analysis was performed or text changed until the relevant artifact has been inspected.

Answer the concern directly, state the actual action or evidence-based reason for not making the requested change, and identify the supporting result or manuscript location. Do not agree with a factual error merely to sound courteous, or accuse the reviewer of not reading. If a point was present in the original, distinguish that clarification from a newly added result. Preserve material unresolved disagreement; neither reviewer preference nor a draft reply establishes scientific correctness.

Follow the user and current portal instructions for combined or separate response files and change-marking style. Keep replies understandable without requiring access to another reviewer's comments. Use the submitted version as the baseline for any marked manuscript. Coordinate substantive changes through the relevant section owner; keep detailed rebuttal reasoning in correspondence or SI unless readers need it to interpret the result.

## Reconcile the deliverables

For the files actually requested, check that every comment has an identifiable answer and every claimed change has evidence. Match quoted revised passages to the current manuscript, distinguishing exact quotations from clearly labelled paraphrases. Verify display numbers and page/line locations against the current compiled or rendered version; never invent locations.

Where clean and marked copies are required, compare their accepted text after accounting for insertion/deletion markup, and inspect the rendered treatment of changes. A clean copy must not retain deleted text or unintended revision marks. Refresh affected quotes, counts and locations after later edits or reflow; do not rerun unrelated scientific checks just because pagination changed. Report correspondence completeness, verified changes, package consistency, rendering and remaining author facts separately. A polished letter or a successful build does not establish submission readiness.
