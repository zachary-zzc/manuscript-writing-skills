# Manuscript Abstract and title

Work on the requested title, Abstract or both within the accepted scientific argument. Preserve bounded scope: a local revision, translation or polish does not require rebuilding the section or paper. Generic prose polishing is handled by [manuscript-polishing](../manuscript-polishing/SKILL.md); whole-manuscript coordination is handled by [manuscript-workflow](../manuscript-workflow/SKILL.md).

## Hard rules for manuscript narrative

Apply the **Editorial Guideline (mandatory default)** in [release narrative and defensive-writing rules](../manuscript-workflow/references/release-narrative-rules.md) before drafting, restructuring, polishing, auditing, or reviewing. They are hard defaults, with special priority for manuscript writing, storyline, idea framing, and innovation claims. When the user supplies manuscript text, perform the required point-by-point defensive-writing audit and give a concrete rewrite for every instance. Preserve material limitations, negative findings, uncertainty, ethics, safety, and reproducibility boundaries as precise evidence or scope statements.

For Nature or Nature Portfolio targets, follow the [canonical Nature integration map](../manuscript-workflow/references/nature-integration.md) and load only the relevant `nature-shared` reference; this manuscript skill remains the owner.

Read the shared [interaction contract](../manuscript-workflow/references/contract.md) directly before work. Reuse the task’s confirmed Easy, Normal or Expert mode and accepted decisions; do not invoke the coordinator or repeat intake merely because the section skill changes.

Read [section.md](references/section.md), the shared [writing style](../manuscript-workflow/references/writing-style.md), and the scoped [paragraph review](../manuscript-workflow/references/paragraph-review.md). Apply full-draft requirements only at their stated scope; the section reference preserves author overrides and binding-format conflicts.

Use the accepted contribution, strongest supported findings, actual study conditions and target format. For full-manuscript production, finalise the Abstract and title only after H7–H10 stabilize the Results, figures, Introduction, Methods, Supplementary Information and Discussion. Use the shared terminology table for headline terms, metrics, conditions and claim strength. A provisional Abstract must be explicitly labelled and cannot satisfy H11. Reconcile title and headline claims with the body before finalising the requested text.

Verify the title claim map, headline numbers and conclusion against Results; check the applicable Abstract length and format from the section reference. Apply the contract’s review checkpoints to the requested unit, recheck affected dependencies and report only the verification actually completed.
