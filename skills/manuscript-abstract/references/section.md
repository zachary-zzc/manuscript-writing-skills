### Title

Identify the subject, meaningful advance and actual scope directly. Prefer a concrete action to vague promises. Avoid promotional claims, unexplained labels and the routine colon-title template. Do not elevate a tentative hypothesis into a validated mechanism or foreground a secondary engineering property over the accepted contribution.

**Gate:** each substantive title claim maps to decisive evidence; the title agrees with the Abstract and Results. Length limits follow the actual target.

### Abstract

For full research-manuscript drafts, the author's default is an Abstract of **more than 200 words**. Develop the problem, study approach, central result, decisive evidence and supported implication sufficiently to meet this length without filler or a catalogue of secondary analyses. A later explicit author instruction can set a different length. If a binding submission format conflicts, identify the conflict and distinguish the full working draft from an expressly requested submission-length version; do not silently substitute a shorter generic journal default.

Build one continuous argument from background and problem to contribution, principal findings and significance. A single paragraph is the default for a research article when compatible with its format. Provide enough problem context before scores. Avoid an architecture catalogue, abrupt topic switching and unexplained evaluation jargon.

Select concrete results that establish central claims, identifying the measured object, metric and comparison where quantitative. Avoid both vague success statements and an inventory of numbers. Distinguish reconstruction or verification of known analyses from question-driven investigation, and separate studies whose juxtaposition could imply one discovery pipeline. Where human intervention is an actual study condition contributing to a headline result, identify that role. Omit fine-grained validation procedures unless they are the headline innovation. Mention resource scale only when essential to the present contribution, with credit intact. Include a boundary here when the headline would otherwise mislead, not every implementation constraint.

**Gate:** a nonspecialist can explain the need, advance and strongest evidence without opening Methods. Every headline number and conclusion agrees with the body.
